Sri Panchayudha Stotram

sphuratsahasrāraśikhātitīvraṃ
sudarśanaṃ bhāskarakōṭitulyam ।
suradviṣāṃ prāṇavināśi viṣṇōḥ
chakraṃ sadā'haṃ śaraṇaṃ prapadyē ॥ 1 ॥


viṣṇōrmukhōtthānilapūritasya
yasya dhvanirdānavadarpahantā ।
taṃ pāñchajanyaṃ śaśikōṭiśubhraṃ
śaṅkhaṃ sadā'haṃ śaraṇaṃ prapadyē ॥ 2 ॥


hiraṇmayīṃ mērusamānasārāṃ
kaumōdakīṃ daityakulaikahantrīm ।
vaikuṇṭhavāmāgrakarāgramṛṣṭāṃ
gadāṃ sadā'haṃ śaraṇaṃ prapadyē ॥ 3 ॥


yajjyāninādaśravaṇātsurāṇāṃ
chētāṃsi nirmuktabhayāni sadyaḥ ।
bhavanti daityāśanibāṇavarṣaiḥ
śārṅgaṃ sadā'haṃ śaraṇaṃ prapadyē ॥ 4 ॥


rakṣō'surāṇāṃ kaṭhinōgrakaṇṭha-
-chChēdakṣarat‍kṣōṇita digdhasāram ।
taṃ nandakaṃ nāma harēḥ pradīptaṃ
khaḍgaṃ sadā'haṃ śaraṇaṃ prapadyē ॥ 5 ॥


imaṃ harēḥ pañchamahāyudhānāṃ
stavaṃ paṭhēdyō'nudinaṃ prabhātē ।
samasta duḥkhāni bhayāni sadyaḥ
pāpāni naśyanti sukhāni santi ॥ 6 ॥


vanē raṇē śatru jalāgnimadhyē
yadṛchChayāpatsu mahābhayēṣu ।
paṭhētvidaṃ stōtramanākulātmā
sukhībhavēttatkṛta sarvarakṣaḥ ॥ 7 ॥


sachakraśaṅkhaṃ sagadāsiśārṅgaṃ
[yachchakraśaṅkhaṃ gadakhaḍgaśārṅgiṇaṃ]
pītāmbaraṃ kaustubha-vatsa-chihnam । [kaustubha-vatsa-lāñChitam]
śriyā samētōjjvala śōbhitāṅgaṃ
viṣṇuṃ sadā'haṃ śaraṇaṃ prapadyē ॥



jalē rakṣatu vārāhaḥ sthalē rakṣatu vāmanaḥ ।
aṭavyāṃ nārasiṃhaścha sarvataḥ pātu kēśavaḥ ॥


iti pañchāyudha stōtram ॥ ॥
