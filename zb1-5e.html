Ramayana Jaya Mantram

jayatyatibalō rāmō lakṣmaṇaścha mahābalaḥ
rājā jayati sugrīvō rāghavēṇābhipālitaḥ ।
dāsōhaṃ kōsalēndrasya rāmasyākliṣṭakarmaṇaḥ
hanumān śatrusainyānāṃ nihantā mārutātmajaḥ ॥

na rāvaṇa sahasraṃ mē yuddhē pratibalaṃ bhavēt
śilābhistu praharataḥ pādapaiścha sahasraśaḥ ।
ardhayitvā purīṃ laṅkāmabhivādya cha maithilīṃ
samṛddhārdhō gamiṣyāmi miṣatāṃ sarvarakṣasām ॥



Makutadharanam Sarga

bramanaa nirmitam poorvam kiritam ratnasobhitam |
abishiktah puraa yena manustam deeptatejasam ||

tasyanwavaaye rajaanah cramadyenabishechitaah |
sabhayaam hemakluptayaam sobhithayaam mahaadhanih |
ratnairnanaavidhaischaiva chitrithayaam susobhanih |
naanaratnamaye pithe kalpayitvaa yathavidhi ||

kiritena tatah pachaadvasisthena mahatmana |
rutvigbhirbhusanaischaiva samayokshyata raghavah ||
